Carel PJ Easy (PJEZ) · EtC

What does EtC mean on a Carel PJ Easy walk-in cooler?

Short answer: Clock alarm. If your Carel PJ Easy (PJEZ) is showing EtC, the controller has logged a specific, documented condition. This page covers what it means, what causes it, what you can safely fix yourself, and when — and why — it takes a technician.

What exactly is the machine telling you?

On PJEZ models with a real-time clock, the clock has failed or lost its setting, so time-of-day defrost bands won't run correctly.

Which machines show this?

The Carel PJ Easy (PJEZ) is a small, low-cost panel-mount controller that box and case OEMs fit widely to coolers, freezers, and walk-in panels, and that contractors commonly install as a replacement thermostat. It uses short two- or three-character codes that alternate with the temperature reading: E0/E1/E2 for failed probes, HI/LO for temperature alarms, dOR (shown as 'dr' on some models) for the door, cht/CHt for a dirty condenser, EE/EF for parameter memory errors. Practical tip: dF flashing on the display just means a defrost is running — it's normal status, not a fault, so wait out the cycle before assuming something is wrong.

What causes it?

  • On PJEZ models with a real-time clock, the clock failed or lost its setting — often after a power outage
  • Time-of-day defrost bands won't run correctly until it's reset

Can you fix it yourself?

Sometimes. After an outage, a trained operator re-setting the clock parameters may resolve it. Verify scheduled defrosts still occur at their normal times.

Step by step — what to try, in order:

  1. Check for a recent power outage
  2. Verify scheduled defrosts are still occurring at normal times
  3. Have your trained operator re-set the clock parameters if applicable
One reset is diagnosis, repeated resets are damage. If the same fault returns after a single reset, the underlying condition is real — stop resetting and deal with the cause.

When do you need a technician — and why?

A clock that won't hold time points to failed hardware — the controller may need replacement, and until then the defrost schedule the box depends on isn't running when it should.

When to call: Call your service company if the clock won't hold time — the controller may need replacement.
