Carel Fault & Error Codes

34 documented codes and fault signals, verified against service manuals, with plain-English fixes.

Carel controllers are fitted by walk-in and refrigeration OEMs worldwide — most commonly the ir33 / ir33+ family and the smaller PJ Easy (PJEZ), the compact digital thermostats you'll find running boxes from many different builders. Their code language is compact: E-codes are probe faults (E0 room probe, E1 defrost probe, E2 condenser probe), HI and LO are the temperature alarms, dor/dOR the door alarm, Ed/Ed1 means a defrost timed out before clearing the coil, and the condenser pair — lowercase cht as the dirty-condenser early warning, uppercase CHt as the hard alarm — is the difference between a cleaning reminder and a compressor at risk. A code that fools everyone: dEF or dF isn't a fault at all, just the display's way of saying a defrost is running.

Carel controllers on HACCP-equipped installs also log HA/HF temperature-excursion events. Find your exact code below for the documented meaning, safe checks, and the technician threshold.
