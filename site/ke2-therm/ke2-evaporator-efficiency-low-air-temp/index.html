KE2 Therm KE2 Evaporator Efficiency · LOW AIR TEMP

What does LOW AIR TEMP mean on a KE2 Therm KE2 Evaporator Efficiency walk-in cooler?

Short answer: Room air temperature too cold. If your KE2 Therm KE2 Evaporator Efficiency is showing LOW AIR TEMP, the controller has logged a specific, documented condition. This page covers what it means, what causes it, what you can safely fix yourself, and when — and why — it takes a technician.

What exactly is the machine telling you?

Air temperature has been below the setpoint by the low-temp alarm offset (factory default 4°F below setpoint) for the alarm delay time (default 10 minutes). Yellow LED is lit and the system continues to run.

Can you fix it yourself?

Step by step — what to try, in order:

  1. Check the actual room temperature with a separate thermometer to confirm the reading
  2. Verify the setpoint and the low-temp alarm offset/delay were not accidentally changed
  3. Note whether the alarm clears on its own once temperature returns to range
One reset is diagnosis, repeated resets are damage. If the same fault returns after a single reset, the underlying condition is real — stop resetting and deal with the cause.
Safety: Freezing risk to refrigerated (non-frozen) product.

When do you need a technician — and why?

When to call: Call a tech if the room keeps overcooling, because a leaking liquid line solenoid or misadjusted low-pressure control needs professional service.
