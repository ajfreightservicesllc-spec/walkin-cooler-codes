KE2 Therm Fault & Error Codes

12 documented codes and fault signals, verified against service manuals, with plain-English fixes.

KE2 Therm's Evaporator Efficiency controller is fitted to thousands of walk-ins — sometimes from the factory, often as a retrofit that pays for itself in defrost energy savings. It skips cryptic codes entirely and scrolls named alarms across its display: HIGH AIR TEMP, DOOR SWITCH, EXCESS DEFROST, LOW SUPERHEAT. The LED color is your severity gauge — yellow means the system is still running and you have time to diagnose; a red critical alarm like PRESSURE SENSOR means the controller shut refrigeration down to protect the compressor, and box temperature is now rising on the clock.

Two KE2-specific things worth knowing. First, the VARIABLES menu turns sensor alarms into instant diagnosis: a reading of -87 means the sensor is open or unplugged, 180+ means it's shorted. Second, several KE2 alarms are really system diagnoses — LOW SUPERHEAT most commonly means the compressor never started (frequently a low-pressure control that was never field-set, which shows up as the weather cools each fall). Find your exact alarm below for what it means, what to check, and when to call.
