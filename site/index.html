Find your controller

The brand on the box door usually isn't the brand talking — the alarm comes from the refrigeration controller. Match what's on your display:

Carel ir33 / ir33+
Compact panel display; the code shows in place of the temperature with a bell icon.
Carel PJ Easy (PJEZ)
Small Carel thermostat; E0/E1/E2, dOr and cht/CHt alternate with the temperature.
Danfoss ERC 211 / 213 / 214
Danfoss ERC 21X display; E27/E29/E30 sensor errors and A01–A99 alarms.
Danfoss EKC 202 / AK-CC 210
Danfoss case and cold-room controller; E, A and S (status) codes.
Danfoss Optyma Plus
Controller built into a Danfoss condensing unit; A2, A17, A80, A96.
Dixell XR06CX / XR60CX
Small Dixell XR display on many Norlake and Kolpak boxes; P1, HA, LA, dA, CA.
Heatcraft intelliGen
OLED display with a green / yellow / red light bar; AL, ER and IN codes.
Heatcraft Beacon II
Two-digit LED on the evaporator board (A1–A4, E1–E9); optional LCD Smart Controller.
KE2 Evaporator Efficiency
Scrolls alarms in plain words with a yellow or red LED beside the readout.
Norlake programmable controller
Four-line LCD panel with an ALARM button; alarms in full sentences.
