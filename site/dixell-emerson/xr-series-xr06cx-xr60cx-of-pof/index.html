Dixell (Emerson) XR-series (XR06CX/XR60CX) · OF / PoF

Why does my Dixell controller say OF or PoF when I press a button?

Short answer: Keyboard locked (not a fault). If your Dixell (Emerson) XR-series (XR06CX/XR60CX) is showing OF / PoF, the controller has logged a specific, documented condition. This page covers what it means, what causes it, what you can safely fix yourself, and when — and why — it takes a technician.

What exactly is the machine telling you?

"OF" (XR06CX) or "PoF" (XR60CX) appears when a key is pressed while the keypad is locked. It is not an alarm — the lock prevents accidental setpoint changes. Unlock by holding the UP and DOWN arrow keys together for more than 3 seconds until "on"/"Pon" is displayed.

Which machines show this?

Dixell's XR series (now under Emerson/Copeland) is probably the most widely installed walk-in thermostat family there is. The small panel-mount XR06CX and XR60CX ship as the standard controller on boxes from Norlake, Kolpak, and many other walk-in manufacturers, and they're a favorite replacement control when an original thermostat dies. The display normally shows box temperature; when something's wrong, a short two- or three-character code flashes or alternates with the temperature — P-codes for failed probes, HA/LA for temperature alarms, dA for the door, CA for a shutdown-level fault. Practical tip: most alarms silence when you press any key but keep displaying until the condition clears — so a code still showing means the problem is still live.

What causes it?

  • The keypad is locked and someone pressed a key — this is a status message, not a fault
  • The lock exists precisely to stop accidental setpoint changes

Can you fix it yourself?

Completely. Hold the UP and DOWN arrows together for more than 3 seconds until the display shows "on" (XR06CX) or "Pon" (XR60CX) — unlocked. Nothing about refrigeration has changed.

Step by step — what to try, in order:

  1. Hold the UP and DOWN arrow keys together for over 3 seconds to unlock
  2. The display shows "on" (XR06CX) or "Pon" (XR60CX) when unlocked
One reset is diagnosis, repeated resets are damage. If the same fault returns after a single reset, the underlying condition is real — stop resetting and deal with the cause.

When do you need a technician — and why?

The only scenario needing service is a keypad that refuses to unlock after the correct hold, which points to a failed keypad or controller.

When to call: No service call needed unless the keypad won't unlock, which may indicate a failed keypad or controller.
