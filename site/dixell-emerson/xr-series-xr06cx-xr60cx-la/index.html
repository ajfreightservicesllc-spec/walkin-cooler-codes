Dixell (Emerson) XR-series (XR06CX/XR60CX) · LA

What does LA mean on a Dixell (Emerson) XR-series?

Short answer: Minimum (low) temperature alarm. If your Dixell (Emerson) XR-series (XR06CX/XR60CX) is showing LA, the controller has logged a specific, documented condition. This page covers what it means, what causes it, what you can safely fix yourself, and when — and why — it takes a technician.

What exactly is the machine telling you?

Room temperature dropped to the minimum temperature alarm setpoint (AL/ALL parameter) past the alarm delay. Outputs are unchanged, and the alarm stops automatically when temperature returns to normal.

Can you fix it yourself?

Step by step — what to try, in order:

  1. Verify actual box temperature with a separate thermometer
  2. Check whether the setpoint was recently changed
  3. Move freeze-sensitive product away from the evaporator discharge if the box is overcooling
One reset is diagnosis, repeated resets are damage. If the same fault returns after a single reset, the underlying condition is real — stop resetting and deal with the cause.
Safety: Freezing risk to refrigerated product.

When do you need a technician — and why?

When to call: Call a tech if the box keeps running colder than setpoint, which can indicate a stuck contactor or control problem.
