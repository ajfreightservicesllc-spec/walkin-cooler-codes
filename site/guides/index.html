Troubleshooting, maintenance, and buying guidance for the people who keep walk-in coolers running.

Why is my walk-in cooler not cooling or holding temperature?
Six causes explain most warm walk-ins. Three of them are free to fix, and product is on the clock.
What do the alarm codes on my walk-in cooler mean?
The box doesn't throw codes — the controller does. Identify yours, then learn what it's actually measuring.
Why is ice building up in my walk-in freezer or cooler?
Every patch of ice in a walk-in is moisture that got in and a defrost that didn't handle it. Find the source.
How do you maintain a commercial walk-in cooler or freezer?
Walk-ins fail from neglect in predictable ways. A daily/weekly/quarterly rhythm that catches all of them early.
How cold should a walk-in cooler or freezer be?
Coolers: food at 41°F or below, so most boxes run 35-38°F. Freezers: 0°F or below. Here's why, and how to check yours honestly.
Why does my walk-in cooler run constantly and never shut off?
A compressor that never cycles off is fighting heat, starving for refrigerant, or being lied to by a sensor.
Why is there water on the floor of my walk-in cooler?
Four sources explain walk-in floor water: the drain, the door, the pan, or a leak. Trace it before the injury or the citation.
