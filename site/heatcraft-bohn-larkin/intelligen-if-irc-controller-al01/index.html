Heatcraft (Bohn/Larkin) intelliGen iF/iRC controller · AL01

What does AL01 mean on a Heatcraft (Bohn/Larkin) intelliGen iF/iRC controller?

Short answer: High box temperature alarm. If your Heatcraft (Bohn/Larkin) intelliGen iF/iRC controller is showing AL01, the controller has logged a specific, documented condition. This page covers what it means, what causes it, what you can safely fix yourself, and when — and why — it takes a technician.

What exactly is the machine telling you?

The walk-in box temperature has stayed above the programmed High Box Temperature alarm setpoint (default 50F cooler / 40F freezer) longer than the alarm delay time (default 60 minutes). The light bar turns red and AL01 shows on the OLED display.

Can you fix it yourself?

Step by step — what to try, in order:

  1. Check that all doors are fully closed and door gaskets seal tight
  2. Ask staff whether product was just loaded — warm product can trip this alarm temporarily
  3. Look at the evaporator coil inside the box for heavy ice buildup
  4. Check the outdoor/condensing unit: breaker on, fans spinning, coil free of dirt, leaves and boxes blocking airflow
  5. Verify no one left the unit in Service or Product Load mode
One reset is diagnosis, repeated resets are damage. If the same fault returns after a single reset, the underlying condition is real — stop resetting and deal with the cause.

When do you need a technician — and why?

When to call: If temperature does not start falling within an hour after these checks, call a refrigeration technician — product is at risk.
