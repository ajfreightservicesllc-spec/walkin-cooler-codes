Danfoss Fault & Error Codes

30 documented codes and fault signals, verified against service manuals, with plain-English fixes.

Danfoss electronic controllers run an enormous share of commercial refrigeration, and several of its families end up on walk-in coolers and their condensing units: the ERC 211/213/214 controllers found on cabinets and walk-in control panels, the EKC 202 and AK-CC 210/250 case and room controllers, and the Optyma Plus controller mounted on Danfoss condensing units that serve walk-ins. The code language is consistent across them: E-codes are sensor and electronics errors (E29 is the air sensor, E27 the defrost sensor), A-codes are alarms — temperature (A01/A02 or A1/A2), door (A04/A4), voltage protection (A99/AA1), and the condenser pair worth memorizing: A61 warns the condenser is running hot, A80 is the blocked-condenser lockout that requires a manual reset after you clean the coil.

Some AK-CC models add HACCP logging (A60) that timestamps food-safety excursions. Find your controller family and exact code below for the documented meaning, the safe checks, and when it's a service call.
